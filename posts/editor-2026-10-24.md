The first release of the modder's toolkit: everything the game acts on is now a plain-text file, and this is the reference and the tool for them.

**New since the 24 October (f) playtest build**
- The game's content moved out of code into record files under `Doctrines_Data/StreamingAssets/Data`: items (weapons, shields, armour, kit, relics, mounts), peoples (the stock the men are raised from; new races are files), the units on the muster rolls, the doctrine presets, the AI's army archetypes, point costs, weather, roads and the march, scouting, the clock, relief, cover and world generation, and the pose files for how weapons and horses are drawn.
- Mods: a folder under `Mods/` with the same layout. A record with a base id replaces it, a new id adds. Several mods load in name order.
- F9 in the running game reloads every file; problems are listed in `output_log.txt` with file and line.
- One record format for every file: `item sword` at the margin, indented `key value` fields, `#` comments, quotes for names with spaces.
- Firearms and crossbows gained three `ranged` fields, all 0..1: `armour-piercing` (the share of the struck armour's protection the missile ignores), `shield-piercing` (the share of a shield's toughness that does not count) and `stopping` (the least chance a hit puts the man down whatever he wears). The musket is 0.7/0.9/0.8, the arquebus 0.5/0.7/0.6, the crossbow 0.3/0.4/0.25; bows are 0.

**In the zip**
- `doctrines-editor.exe`: `check` lists every problem in your files, `list` and `show <id>` print what the game sees (a unit with its computed load, speed, protection and points), `new-mod <name>` scaffolds a mod folder. Run it from the game folder or set `DOCTRINES_DATA`.
- `Modding/`: the reference. `README.md` first, then a `SKILL.md` per domain: equipment, peoples, units and doctrines, armies and AI, rules and weather, poses and animation, maps and terrain, audio and art, tools and checks. Each lists every field, its unit and what the sim does with it, with worked examples, and says what is still code and what is planned.

**Still code, on the list**: camp, rout and officer constants; fire-drill parameters; sounds loaded from files; hand-made battlefields and maps; factions; the peoples' appearance.
